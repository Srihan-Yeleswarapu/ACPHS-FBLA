What is FBLA-IT?

What is FBLA?

Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) is the largest student business organization in the country. Members build leadership skills, compete in business and technology events, and connect with other students.

What is FBLA-IT?

FBLA-IT is ACP High School's FBLA chapter. We bring together students interested in business, leadership, and technology through meetings, competitions, and chapter activities.

What to expect

Regular chapter meetings, competitive-event training, conference opportunities, and a chance to build skills that last beyond high school.
