# About Page

Alright so this is the plan for the about page. Its the page people hit when they want to know what FBLA-IT actually is, so it should be short and easy to read, not a wall of text. Heres what goes on it, top to bottom.

First, the intro. A quick explainer of the club. FBLA-IT is the Future Business Leaders of America chapter at ACP High School, and basically its the club for anyone whos even a little into business, leadership, or tech. The whole vibe of this part should be that you dont need to be a business kid to join and you dont need to know anything going in. You show up, you hang out, you find what sounds interesting, and the club helps you go from there. Keep it friendly and not formal.

Next, a quote from the advisor. We want a short thing our advisor has to say about the club, with their name and title under it. I dont have the real quote yet, so thats marked Content in Progress until they send it over.

Then the achievements section. This is the show off part. A few pictures of stuff the chapter has done, like trophies, conference trips, club fair setups, that kind of thing. Each picture gets a short caption under it so people know what theyre looking at. The photos arent in yet either, so that part is Content in Progress for now.

Last is the officers. A simple list of who runs the club and what each one actually does. President runs the meetings and keeps the chapter moving, the Vice President covers when the president cant make it and helps plan events, the Secretary takes notes and keeps announcements posted, the Treasurer handles dues and the money stuff, and whoever runs Social Media posts about meetings and events so people actually show up. Real names go in once the officer lineup is final.

Thats the whole page. Nothing fancy, just the basics so anyone new gets the idea without having to ask.
