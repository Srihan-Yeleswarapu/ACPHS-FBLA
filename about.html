What is FBLA-IT?

FBLA-IT is the Future Business Leaders of America chapter at ACP High School. Basically, it's the club for anyone who's even a little into business, leadership, or tech.

Here's the deal though. You don't need to be a business kid to join, and you don't need to know anything going in either. You show up, you hang out, you find what sounds interesting, and the club helps you go from there.

Every year we compete against other schools in competitive events, go to the state conference in the spring, and the people who qualify make it to nationals in the summer. Some weeks it's a lot of work. Most weeks it's just fun. And if you care about college apps, the resume boost doesn't hurt.
